The club

One club. Every discipline.

A prism splits one beam of white light into a full spectrum. We want to do the same with curiosity. Project PRISM brings students from every school together to turn their questions into real research.

Sciences · Engineering · Pharmacy · Psychology · and more

PRISM is a codename. The club's official name and identity will be revealed at the inauguration, and founding members will be part of the reveal.

Start with a question

Curiosity is the only prerequisite.

Think across fields

The best ideas live between disciplines.

Learn by doing

Workshops, mentors and real projects.

Leave your mark

Build a research portfolio before you graduate.
